Our Story

From Humble Beginnings to Industry Leaders

Stackly was founded in 2009 by a team of real estate professionals who saw a gap in the property management industry. What started as a small operation managing a handful of properties has grown into a nationally recognized firm overseeing thousands of residential and commercial properties.

Our mission has remained the same: to provide property owners with peace of mind through transparent, efficient, and result-driven management services.

  • Founded with a passion for real estate excellence
  • Grew from 50 to 2,500+ properties under management
  • Expanded to 12 metropolitan areas nationwide
  • Winner of multiple industry excellence awards

Our Mission

To empower property owners with comprehensive, technology-driven management solutions that maximize returns, minimize stress, and create lasting value for every stakeholder involved.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted and innovative property management company in the nation, setting the gold standard for service quality, transparency, and client satisfaction in real estate management.
