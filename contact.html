How do I get started with Stackly?

Getting started is easy! Simply fill out the contact form on this page or call us directly. We'll schedule a free consultation to assess your property and discuss your management needs. There's no obligation, and we'll provide a customized proposal within 48 hours.

What areas do you serve?

We are headquartered in Salem, Tamil Nadu and proudly serve property owners across Salem and surrounding regions in Tamil Nadu. We manage residential, commercial, and mixed-use properties, and are always expanding to new localities.

Can I transfer my existing property management to Stackly?

Absolutely! We handle seamless transitions from other management companies. Our team manages the entire transfer process including tenant notifications, documentation transfer, and onboarding, with minimal disruption to your tenants and income.

Do you manage commercial properties?

Yes, we manage both residential and commercial properties. Our commercial management services include office spaces, retail locations, and mixed-use buildings. Contact us for a customized commercial management proposal.
