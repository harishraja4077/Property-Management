FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our property management services

What percentage do you charge for property management?

Our management fees vary by plan. The Basic plan starts at $149/month, Professional at $299/month, and Enterprise at $499/month. For larger portfolios, we offer custom pricing. All plans include no hidden fees and transparent billing.

How quickly can you find a tenant for my property?

On average, we find qualified tenants within 7-14 days. Our extensive marketing network and database of pre-screened tenants allows us to minimize vacancy periods significantly compared to industry averages.

Do you handle maintenance and repairs?

Yes, we handle all aspects of property maintenance. We have a network of vetted, licensed contractors who provide competitive pricing. All maintenance requests are tracked through our platform, and owners receive detailed reports.

How often will I receive financial reports?

All plans include monthly financial statements. Professional and Enterprise plans offer real-time dashboard access where you can view income, expenses, and property performance anytime. Annual tax summaries are also provided.

What happens if a tenant stops paying rent?

We have a structured process for handling late payments including automated reminders, personal contact, and if necessary, formal legal notices. Our Enterprise plan includes full eviction management with court representation.
